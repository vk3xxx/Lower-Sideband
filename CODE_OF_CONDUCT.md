# Community conduct

Lower Sideband welcomes constructive participation from people of all backgrounds and experience levels.

Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and focus criticism on ideas and code;
- welcome questions and differing technical perspectives;
- respect privacy and never publish another person's identity, messages, location, or credentials;
- avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualised attention, and disruptive behaviour;
- disclose conflicts of interest when reviewing or recommending changes.

Project maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the community or users.

Report conduct concerns privately to **sepus@hotmail.com**. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
